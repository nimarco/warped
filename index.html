            How to play

            1. Press play. You'll hear 10 seconds of a song, badly warped. Replay it as often as you like.
            2. Guess the song. Search by title or artist and pick from the list. Each track has one kind of damage, and each wrong guess or skip eases it a step until try 5 plays the real song: Fast-forward too fast and too high Rewind chopped into backwards pieces Rickrolled Never Gonna Give You Up taped over it Chewed a dog got to the tape Warp the pitch cracks like a breaking voice Muffled through a wall, on a crackly tape
            3. Score. 250 points on try 1, then 200, 150, 100 and 50 on the clean song. Stuck? Peeking at the blurry cover costs 25% of the track's points, and the music video another 50%.
